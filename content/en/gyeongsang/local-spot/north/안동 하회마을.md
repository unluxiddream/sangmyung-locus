Andong Hahoe Folk Village in Andong, Gyeongsangbuk-do, is a historic settlement of traditional hanok surrounded by a beautiful bend in the Nakdong River. Its Joseon-era aristocratic lifestyle and architecture are well preserved, and it is known as the ancestral village of the Pungsan Ryu clan. Visitors can encounter traditions such as the Hahoe Byeolsingut Mask Dance. This UNESCO World Heritage village is an outstanding destination for experiencing Korean traditional life and picturesque scenery.
