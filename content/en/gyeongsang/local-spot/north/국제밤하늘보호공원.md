Yeongyang International Dark Sky Park in Subi-myeon, Yeongyang-gun, Gyeongsangbuk-do, is prized for its exceptionally dark skies, largely free from artificial light pollution. Certified by the International Dark-Sky Association, it offers views of countless stars and the Milky Way on clear nights. Enjoy nature during the day, then experience a breathtaking night sky rarely visible in cities. The combination of quiet surroundings and the vast universe makes for a memorable visit.
