Buseoksa Temple, on the slopes of Bonghwangsan Mountain in Yeongju, Gyeongsangbuk-do, is an ancient Buddhist temple traditionally said to have been founded by Master Uisang during the Silla period. Its cultural treasures include Muryangsujeon Hall, a designated National Treasure and a masterpiece of traditional Korean architecture. The temple grounds offer peaceful views over layers of mountain ridges. As part of the UNESCO World Heritage listing Sansa, Buddhist Mountain Monasteries in Korea, it invites visitors to enjoy centuries of history and natural serenity.
