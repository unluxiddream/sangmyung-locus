Seongbak Forest in Seongju-gun, Gyeongsangbuk-do, is known for its old willow trees that form a beautiful woodland. Having stood for hundreds of years, these trees provide generous shade and a comfortable retreat for residents and travelers. In summer, purple lilyturf flowers spread beneath the trees, creating especially lovely scenery. It is a peaceful place to walk and appreciate the grandeur of ancient trees and the colors of the seasons.
