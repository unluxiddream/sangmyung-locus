Mungyeong Jungang Market in Mungyeong, Gyeongsangbuk-do, is a place to experience daily local life and the energy of a traditional market. Vendors sell fresh produce, prepared foods, and household goods along its alleys. Regional specialties, simple market snacks, and friendly merchants give visitors plenty to explore. The market offers a different, down-to-earth side of Mungyeong beyond its major tourist attractions.
