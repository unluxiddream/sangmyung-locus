The Andong International Mask Dance Festival in Andong, Gyeongsangbuk-do, brings together traditional Korean mask dances and mask cultures from around the world. Korean performances, international troupes, and street parades turn the city into a lively festival space. Visitors can join mask-making and mask-dance lessons as well as watch the shows. Traditional and contemporary performances offer entertainment for all ages. It is an autumn celebration of Andong's culture and artistic traditions from across the globe.
