Jomunguk Museum in Geumseong-myeon, Uiseong-gun, Gyeongsangbuk-do, introduces the history and culture of Jomunguk, an ancient polity believed to have existed in Uiseong during the Samhan period. Artifacts from regional tombs and archaeological sites illustrate the lives and customs of people in that era. Visiting the nearby burial mounds makes the area's ancient past feel even more tangible. This museum is an intriguing destination for discovering the story of a lesser-known kingdom.
