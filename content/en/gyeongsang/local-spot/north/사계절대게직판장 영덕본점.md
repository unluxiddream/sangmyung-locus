Sagyejeol Snow Crab Direct Sales Center, located on Snow Crab Street near Ganggu Port in Yeongdeok-gun, specializes in crab dishes. Steamed snow crab and multi-course seafood meals are its main offerings. Crab-shell fried rice and seafood ramen make popular additions. It is a good place to sample Yeongdeok's best-known specialty while enjoying views of the fishing harbor. Crab varieties and market prices change by season, so check before ordering.
