Ganggu Port in Yeongdeok-gun, Gyeongsangbuk-do, combines the blue East Sea with the lively scenes of a working fishing harbor. Famous as a major center for Yeongdeok snow crab, the waterfront is lined with restaurants offering fresh seafood and crab dishes. Stroll along the harbor to see fishing boats against the open sea. The nearby Yeongdeok Blue Road offers further opportunities to enjoy the coastline. It is a memorable destination for marine scenery, regional flavors, and everyday fishing village life.
