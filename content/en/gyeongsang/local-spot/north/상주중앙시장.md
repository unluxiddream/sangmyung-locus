Sangju Jungang Market in Sangju, Gyeongsangbuk-do, is a lively place to experience the region's everyday culture. Stalls offer locally grown produce, fresh ingredients, and a range of prepared foods. The market provides a close look at the agricultural traditions for which Sangju is known. Friendly vendors and unpretentious alleyways create a warm, welcoming atmosphere.
