The Nakdonggang National Institute of Biological Resources in Sangju, Gyeongsangbuk-do, is a science and education center exploring freshwater biodiversity and its ecological importance. Exhibitions and learning programs introduce species living in the Nakdong River and other Korean freshwater habitats. Its accessible presentations make the natural world and the value of ecosystems engaging for families. The center offers a meaningful way to connect science, nature, and environmental conservation.
