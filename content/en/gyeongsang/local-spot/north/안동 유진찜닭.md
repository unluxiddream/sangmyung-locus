Andong Yujin Jjimdak, in Jjimdak Alley at Andong Old Market, serves Andong's signature braised chicken dish. Generous portions combine chicken, potatoes, and glass noodles in a savory soy-based sauce. Other choices include simmered chicken and garlic chicken. The surrounding market stalls are worth exploring before or after your meal. It is a great place to experience both Andong's regional cuisine and its lively market culture.
