Bohyeonsan Astronomical Science Museum, located at the foot of Bohyeonsan Mountain in Yeongcheon, Gyeongsangbuk-do, is an interactive science center that sparks curiosity about astronomy and space. Observing celestial objects and joining astronomy programs makes stars, planets, and other cosmic phenomena fascinating to explore. The area's clean natural environment provides an appealing setting for night-sky observation. Enjoy the mountain scenery while discovering the mysteries of the universe.
