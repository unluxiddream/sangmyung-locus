Maebongsan Restaurant in Mojeon-dong, Mungyeong, specializes in joksal-jjigae, a local pork stew. Made with Mungyeong's mineral-fed Yakdol pork, the dish brings meat and vegetables together in a hearty meal. Another signature item is the miner's lunch box, recalling life in the area's former coal-mining communities. Traditional Korean food and local stories come together at this friendly restaurant.
