Sosuseowon Confucian Academy in Sunheung-myeon, Yeongju, Gyeongsangbuk-do, was Korea's first royally chartered Confucian academy and a center of Joseon-era education and scholarly culture. Historic buildings for lectures and ancestral rites preserve the layout of a traditional educational institution. Pine forests and serene surroundings complement the academy's elegant atmosphere. Part of the UNESCO World Heritage site Seowon, Korean Neo-Confucian Academies, it offers a chance to reflect on the learning and values of Joseon scholars.
