Daegaya Museum in Goryeong-gun, Gyeongsangbuk-do, explores the history and culture of Daegaya, one of the major kingdoms of the ancient Gaya confederacy. Artifacts unearthed from tombs and historic sites reveal the daily lives and sophisticated culture of its people. Exhibits on distinctive burial customs and tomb construction are especially noteworthy. The museum brings the long-buried stories of Daegaya vividly to life.
