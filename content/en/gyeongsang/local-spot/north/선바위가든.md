Seonbawi Garden in Ibam-myeon, Yeongyang-gun, serves local specialties including set meals featuring wild mountain vegetables. Its dishes highlight plants gathered or grown in the surrounding fields and mountains. The delicate aromas and textures of the greens are a signature feature. It is a good place to discover Yeongyang's clean natural environment and the mountain produce of Irwolsan. Stop here for a wholesome local meal while exploring the area.
