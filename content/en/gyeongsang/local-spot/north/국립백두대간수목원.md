The Baekdudaegan National Arboretum in Bonghwa-gun, Gyeongsangbuk-do, is a vast botanical garden dedicated to conserving the forest ecosystem of the Baekdudaegan mountain range and researching plant resources. Numerous themed gardens showcase different flowers and trees throughout the year. One of its main attractions is the Tiger Forest, where visitors may see endangered Siberian tigers. Exploring forest paths and landscaped gardens offers both relaxation and a deeper appreciation of nature.
