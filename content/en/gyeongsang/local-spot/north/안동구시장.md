Andong Old Market in central Andong, Gyeongsangbuk-do, is a traditional market long intertwined with the lives of local residents. Its Jjimdak Alley, famous for Andong's signature braised chicken, attracts many visitors. Shops offering diverse foods and everyday goods line the aisles, creating a bustling atmosphere. It is one of Andong's signature local attractions for discovering regional food culture and the welcoming charm of a traditional market.
