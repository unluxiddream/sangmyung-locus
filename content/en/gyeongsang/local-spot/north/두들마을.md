Dudeul Village in Seokbo-myeon, Yeongyang-gun, Gyeongsangbuk-do, is a historic community with traditional hanok and centuries-old residences. Known as a settlement of the Jaeryeong Yi clan, it preserves traces of Confucian culture and scholarly life. The village is also closely associated with the literary world of novelist Yi Mun-yol, making it an appealing literary destination. A walk along its quiet lanes and historic houses offers a sense of both tradition and literature.
