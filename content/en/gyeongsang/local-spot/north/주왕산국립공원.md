Juwangsan National Park in Cheongsong-gun, Gyeongsangbuk-do, features dramatic rocky cliffs, deep valleys, and beautiful waterfalls. Unusual rock formations created over long geological periods give Juwangsan its distinctive appearance. Trails following the streams reveal changing scenery in every season. In autumn, brilliant red foliage contrasts with the rugged peaks, making the park especially appealing for hiking and relaxation.
