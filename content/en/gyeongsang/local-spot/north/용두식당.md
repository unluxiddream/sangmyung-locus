Yongdu Restaurant in Bongseong-myeon, Bonghwa-gun, specializes in dishes made with pine mushrooms and neungi mushrooms. Its signature pine mushroom stone-pot rice combines warm rice with a subtle woodland aroma. After the rice, guests can enjoy a delicious crust of scorched rice from the stone pot. Mushroom hot pot and grilled mushroom dishes are also available. The restaurant showcases the flavors of Bonghwa's forests and local specialties in one meal.
