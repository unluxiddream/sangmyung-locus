Seongju Traditional Market in Seongju-gun, Gyeongsangbuk-do, preserves the daily life and food traditions of the community. Visitors can find locally produced fruits and vegetables, including Korean melons, Seongju's best-known specialty. Shops selling simple snacks and household goods make browsing enjoyable. Discover the flavors and friendliness of this region at a market full of local character.
