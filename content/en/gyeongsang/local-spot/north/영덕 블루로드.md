Yeongdeok Blue Road runs along the coast of Yeongdeok-gun, Gyeongsangbuk-do, offering a memorable hiking route with spectacular views of the East Sea. Fishing villages, coastal cliffs, pine forests, and open water create a different scene along each section. Walking the route brings the sound of waves and refreshing sea breezes up close. It is an ideal way to enjoy the blue sea of Yeongdeok at an unhurried pace.
