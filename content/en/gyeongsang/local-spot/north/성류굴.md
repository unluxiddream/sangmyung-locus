Seongnyugul Cave in Geunnam-myeon, Uljin-gun, Gyeongsangbuk-do, is a limestone cave formed over an immense span of geological time. Inside, stalactites, stalagmites, and columns create mysterious underground formations. Illuminated rock features offer a vivid sense of Earth's long natural history. Exploring the cave reveals both geological significance and the wonder of nature at one of Uljin's signature attractions.
