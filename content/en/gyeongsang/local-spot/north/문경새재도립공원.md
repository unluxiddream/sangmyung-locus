Mungyeong Saejae Provincial Park in Mungyeong, Gyeongsangbuk-do, centers on a historic mountain pass that linked the Yeongnam region with Hanyang, the Joseon capital. The old road winding through dense forest and clear streams preserves reminders of Joseon-era travel and history. Three historic gateways, including Juheulgwan, are among its principal landmarks. Follow the footsteps of travelers from centuries past while enjoying refreshing natural scenery.
