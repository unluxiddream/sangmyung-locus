Cheongnyangsan Provincial Park in Bonghwa-gun, Gyeongsangbuk-do, combines striking rock formations, rugged mountain scenery, and deep valleys. The many peaks of Cheongnyangsan form dramatic landscapes with distinctive geological features. Attractions such as Cheongnyangsa Temple and the Sky Bridge connect natural scenery with cultural sites. Visitors can enjoy sweeping views and seasonal mountain colors on a refreshing outdoor trip.
