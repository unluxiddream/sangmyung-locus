The Mungyeong Tea Bowl Festival at Mungyeong Saejae in Gyeongsangbuk-do celebrates the beauty of traditional Korean ceramics. Exhibitions and demonstrations introduce Mungyeong's pottery heritage, including wood-fired kilns and traditional foot-powered potter's wheels. Visitors can participate in tea bowl making and traditional tea ceremonies. Displays and craft markets offer a close look at the skill of local artisans.
