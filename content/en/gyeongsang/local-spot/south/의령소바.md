Uiryeong Soba's main branch, located at Uiryeong Traditional Market in Gyeongsangnam-do, specializes in buckwheat noodles that blend tradition with a modern touch. Drawing on a local dish adapted from Japanese soba introduced during the colonial period, the restaurant has reinterpreted the specialty through two decades of experience. Its freshly made dark buckwheat noodles have a springy texture and nutty aroma that pair well with rich broth. Choose from warm buckwheat noodle soup, chilled soba, or sweet-and-spicy mixed soba throughout the year.
