The Hadong Wild Tea Cultural Festival, held in Hwagae-myeon, Hadong-gun, Gyeongsangnam-do, celebrates the region's long history of tea cultivation. Traditional tea ceremonies and tea-making activities let visitors learn how tea leaves are processed and brewed. Tastings and tea classes led by experts introduce the distinctive flavors and aromas of Hadong's wild tea. Performances, tea exhibitions, and markets selling local specialties add to the festivities. Enjoy a relaxing cup of tea and experience local culture amid the fresh scenery of spring.
