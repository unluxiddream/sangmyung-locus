Sotbawi is a distinctively shaped rock rising from the middle of the Namgang River in Uiryeong-eup, Uiryeong-gun, Gyeongsangnam-do. Its name refers to its resemblance to a cooking pot. A local legend says that great wealth will be born near the rock, making it a symbolic landmark of the area. The rock and surrounding river scenery create a quiet, peaceful setting for enjoying local folklore and Uiryeong's riverside landscape.
