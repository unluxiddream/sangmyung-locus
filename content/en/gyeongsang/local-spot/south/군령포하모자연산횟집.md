On the coast of Samsan-myeon in Goseong-gun, Gullyeongpo Hamo Wild-Caught Sashimi Restaurant is known for dishes featuring pike conger eel, locally called hamo. Visitors can enjoy seasonal seafood, especially hamo sashimi and hamo shabu-shabu. The eel's delicate flavor and tender texture make it the signature ingredient. Other choices include fresh fish sashimi and grilled eel.
