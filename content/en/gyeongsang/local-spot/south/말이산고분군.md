The Marisan Ancient Tombs in Haman-gun, Gyeongsangnam-do, are an important historic site containing the burial mounds of Ara Gaya's ancient ruling class. Large and small mounds stretch across gentle hills, hinting at the prestige of the former kingdom. Objects excavated here provide valuable clues about Ara Gaya's politics, culture, and everyday life. Part of the UNESCO World Heritage site Gaya Tumuli, the scenic burial grounds invite visitors to explore the history of ancient Gaya.
