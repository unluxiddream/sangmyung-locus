Goseong Dinosaur Museum in Hai-myeon, Goseong-gun, Gyeongsangnam-do, is a natural history museum that makes the history and ecology of dinosaurs fascinating to explore. Dinosaur skeleton replicas and fossil collections bring the Mesozoic Era to life. Exhibitions explain different dinosaur species, their characteristics, and the long history of Earth in an accessible way. For a memorable family outing, combine a visit with the nearby dinosaur footprint fossil site at Sangjogam County Park.
