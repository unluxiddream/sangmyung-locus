Geochang Changpowon Garden in Namsang-myeon, Geochang-gun, Gyeongsangnam-do, is an ecological garden with expansive wetlands and diverse plants. As Gyeongsangnam-do's first designated provincial garden, it offers seasonal flowers and beautiful natural scenery. In spring and early summer, irises and other blossoms fill the grounds with color. Walking paths and ecological experience areas make it an ideal place to slow down and enjoy nature.
