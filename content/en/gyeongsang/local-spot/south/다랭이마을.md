Darangee Village in Nam-myeon, Namhae-gun, Gyeongsangnam-do, is a picturesque rural settlement where terraced rice paddies descend steep hillsides toward the blue sea. These stepped fields reflect generations of residents adapting ingeniously to the local terrain. Walking the narrow village lanes and paths between paddies reveals expansive sea views and simple countryside scenes. It is a peaceful place to experience a landscape shaped by nature and human life together.
