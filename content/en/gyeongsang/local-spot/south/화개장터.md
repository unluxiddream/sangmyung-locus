Hwagae Market in Hwagae-myeon, Hadong-gun, Gyeongsangnam-do, is a traditional market near the Seomjingang River. For generations, traders from Gyeongsang and Jeolla gathered here to exchange goods. Stalls selling regional products and foods preserve the market's friendly atmosphere. Hadong tea and mountain vegetables are among the distinctive local specialties. Visitors can appreciate both the cultural links between the Yeongnam and Honam regions and the simple charm of a traditional marketplace.
