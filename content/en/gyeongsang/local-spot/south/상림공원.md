Sangnim Park in Hamyang-gun, Gyeongsangnam-do, traces its origins to a forest said to have been planted by scholar Choe Chi-won during the Silla period to prevent flooding. Trees that have stood here for centuries form a lush, beautiful woodland. Fresh green leaves and colorful autumn foliage make it a popular walking destination throughout the seasons. Enjoy a peaceful stroll along forest paths where a thousand years of history meets nature.
