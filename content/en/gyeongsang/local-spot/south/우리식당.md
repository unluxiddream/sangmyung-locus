Uri Restaurant in Samdong-myeon, Namhae-gun, serves local food, particularly anchovy ssambap, or anchovies eaten in vegetable wraps with rice. Diners can enjoy the ingredients and warm dining traditions of Namhae's fishing villages. The anchovies are simmered with seasoning and leafy greens before being wrapped in fresh vegetables. Seasoned raw anchovies and hairtail dishes provide more ways to sample the area's seafood.
