Donguibogam Village in Geumseo-myeon, Sancheong-gun, Gyeongsangnam-do, is a tourism complex dedicated to traditional Korean medicine and nature. Inspired by Donguibogam, a Joseon-era medical text, and the work of physician Heo Jun, it introduces visitors to traditional healing practices and wellness culture. The Museum of Korean Medicine and various themed facilities explore the history and value of this medical tradition. Set amid the beautiful foothills of Jirisan, it offers relaxation, wellness, and cultural discovery in one visit.
