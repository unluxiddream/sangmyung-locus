Changnyeong Museum in Changnyeong-gun, Gyeongsangnam-do, introduces the area's history and culture. In particular, artifacts excavated from sites and tombs of ancient Bihwa Gaya offer insights into the period's daily life and historical characteristics. Pottery, ornaments, and other objects reveal the rich heritage preserved in Changnyeong. It is a rewarding destination for understanding local history and uncovering traces of Gaya culture.
