Haman Lotus Theme Park in Haman-gun, Gyeongsangnam-do, is an ecological attraction featuring extensive lotus ponds and beautiful natural scenery. Its highlight is the Ara Hongnyeon lotus, revived from ancient lotus seeds discovered at archaeological sites in Haman. In summer, pink blossoms cover the water, creating a peaceful, picturesque landscape. Follow walking paths through the lotuses to appreciate both the area's natural beauty and its remarkable history.
