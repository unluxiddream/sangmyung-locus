Sangjogam County Park in Hai-myeon, Goseong-gun, Gyeongsangnam-do, is famous for its beautiful coastal cliffs and fossilized dinosaur footprints. Waves have sculpted striking rock faces and sea caves over immense spans of time, creating a dramatic landscape. The rocky shore preserves footprints left by dinosaurs during the Cretaceous Period, giving the area considerable geological importance. Discover a remarkable blend of blue southern seas, ancient landscapes, and prehistoric traces.
