Miryang Arirang Market in Miryang, Gyeongsangnam-do, is a traditional market steeped in history and everyday local culture. Shops selling fresh produce, ingredients, and regional foods fill the market alleys with activity. Sample Miryang specialties such as dwaeji-gukbap, a hearty pork-and-rice soup, along with many other local dishes. The friendly vendors and lively market atmosphere offer an authentic glimpse into daily life in Miryang.
