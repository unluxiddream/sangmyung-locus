The Tongyeong Hansan Battle Festival, held in Tongyeong, Gyeongsangnam-do, commemorates the naval victory at the Battle of Hansan and honors Admiral Yi Sun-sin's legacy. A large-scale reenactment of the historic sea battle is the festival's signature spectacle. A procession portraying the naval commander of the Three Provinces, hands-on Joseon naval experiences, and cultural performances bring history to life. Citizen parades and evening events offer something for all generations.
