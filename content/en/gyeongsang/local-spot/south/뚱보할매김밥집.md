Ttungbo Halmae Gimbap in Jungang-dong, Tongyeong, serves Chungmu gimbap, one of the city's signature foods. This local specialty is closely connected to the food traditions of Tongyeong's seafaring communities. Small seaweed-wrapped rice rolls are served separately with spicy seasoned squid and seokbakji, a type of cubed radish kimchi. Simple as it looks, the combination creates a distinctive savory flavor.
