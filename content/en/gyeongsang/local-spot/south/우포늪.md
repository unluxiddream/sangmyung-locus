Upo Wetland in Changnyeong-gun, Gyeongsangnam-do, is one of Korea's best-known inland wetlands and a vital habitat for diverse plants and animals. Aquatic plants, migratory birds, and other wildlife inhabit its vast waters and marshes, creating scenery that changes with every season. Walking the visitor trails brings you close to quiet waters, reed beds, and the thriving wetland ecosystem. Its unspoiled beauty offers both a lesson in ecological conservation and a peaceful escape.
