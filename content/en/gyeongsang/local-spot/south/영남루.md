Yeongnamnu Pavilion in Miryang, Gyeongsangnam-do, is a landmark Joseon-era pavilion overlooking the Miryang River from a hilltop. Renowned for its grand and elegant timber construction, it has long been admired as a scenic destination. The pavilion offers sweeping views of the river and city, evoking the leisurely pursuits of scholars in the past. It is one of Miryang's outstanding cultural treasures, combining history and scenic beauty.
