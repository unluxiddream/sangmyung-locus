Located near Donguibogam Village in Sancheong-gun, Herbs and Mushrooms Valley is a restaurant specializing in local herbs and mushrooms. Its dishes include herb-and-mushroom bibimbap, mushroom shabu-shabu, and spicy fish stew, prepared to highlight the ingredients' natural aromas. It is a good place to enjoy a nourishing meal centered on produce from Sancheong.
