Namhae German Village in Samdong-myeon, Namhae-gun, Gyeongsangnam-do, is known for red-roofed houses and European-inspired architecture. It was established to help Korean miners and nurses who had worked in Germany in the 1960s and 1970s settle after returning home, giving the village a distinctive historical story. German-style buildings, quaint lanes, and views of the blue Namhae Sea evoke a small European town. This destination combines unusual scenery with an important chapter of modern Korean history.
