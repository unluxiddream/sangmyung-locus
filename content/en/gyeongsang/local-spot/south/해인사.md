Haeinsa Temple, nestled on the slopes of Gayasan Mountain in Hapcheon-gun, Gyeongsangnam-do, is a historic Buddhist temple founded during the Silla period and an important center of Korean Buddhism. It is renowned for preserving the Tripitaka Koreana, a collection of woodblocks inscribed on the UNESCO Memory of the World Register. The Janggyeong Panjeon halls housing the woodblocks are also a UNESCO World Heritage site celebrated for their historical and architectural significance. Surrounded by lush forest, the tranquil temple invites visitors to experience a thousand years of Buddhist culture and history.
