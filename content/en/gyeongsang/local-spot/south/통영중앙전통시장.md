Tongyeong Jungang Traditional Market in Tongyeong, Gyeongsangnam-do, offers an up-close look at the city's abundant seafood and everyday local culture. Vendors selling fresh fish, shellfish, and regional specialties fill its aisles with energy. Browsing Tongyeong's signature foods and local products reveals the distinctive food culture of this coastal city. Located near Dongpirang Mural Village, the market is an excellent stop for experiencing Tongyeong's flavors and daily life.
