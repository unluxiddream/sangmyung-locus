Hwangmaesan Mountain, spanning Hapcheon-gun and Sancheong-gun in Gyeongsangnam-do, is renowned for broad ridgelines and beautiful highland grasslands. In spring, masses of pink azaleas blanket the slopes and attract many visitors. In autumn, silver grass sways in the wind, creating a different kind of spectacle. Open views and changing seasonal landscapes make the area a wonderful place for walks and hiking.
