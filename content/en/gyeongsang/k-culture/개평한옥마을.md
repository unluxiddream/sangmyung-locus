Located in Jigok-myeon, Hamyang-gun, Gyeongsangnam-do, Gaepyeong Hanok Village is a historic community where long-standing traditions and scholarly culture live on. Traditional houses, including the former residence of Jeong Yeo-chang, offer a glimpse of an aristocratic family's life during the Joseon Dynasty. Walking past stone walls and tiled roofs feels like stepping back in time. The peaceful, welcoming village is a place to appreciate the beauty of traditional Korean architecture at a leisurely pace.
