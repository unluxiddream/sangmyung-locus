Dongpirang Mural Village in Tongyeong, Gyeongsangnam-do, is an art-filled neighborhood where colorful murals decorate old residential lanes and walls. Once threatened with demolition, the area was revived as a tourist destination through local residents' efforts and public art. Distinctive paintings and charming spots make exploring the alleys a pleasure. From the hilltop, visitors can enjoy views of Tongyeong Port and the surrounding city, blending art with the romance of the sea.
