Located in Danseong-myeon, Sancheong-gun, Gyeongsangnam-do, Namsa Yedam Village is a historic cultural village where traditional hanok houses sit beside old stone-walled lanes. Its preserved houses and streets reflect the scholarly culture of the Joseon Dynasty and generations of village life. Winding stone walls and charming historic homes create a distinctive atmosphere. Wander slowly through the alleys to enjoy the calm and beauty of this traditional village.
