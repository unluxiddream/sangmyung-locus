The Mungyeong Saejae Open Film Set, located inside Mungyeong Saejae Provincial Park in Mungyeong, Gyeongsangbuk-do, is a large historical-drama filming location recreating Joseon-era palaces, streets, and traditional homes. Carefully detailed buildings and period scenery make visitors feel as though they have stepped back into the Joseon Dynasty. Used for numerous films and television dramas, the set is fun to explore while recalling familiar scenes. From grand palace buildings to simple thatched cottages, it offers a glimpse of the era's architecture and everyday life. Surrounded by Mungyeong Saejae's beautiful scenery, it brings together history and screen culture.
