Choi Champan's House in Agyang-myeon, Hadong-gun, Gyeongsangnam-do, is a literary attraction recreating the home of a character in Park Kyong-ni's epic novel Toji (Land). Hanok houses, thatched cottages, and traditional village spaces bring the story's historical setting to life. The wide Pyeongsari fields and scenic Seomjingang River add to the area's peaceful rural charm. Visitors can experience Korean literature and traditional village scenery together.
