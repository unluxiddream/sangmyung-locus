---
info: Gyeongsang-do, a journey through time and scenery
---

From historic villages to blue seas, deep mountains, and lively market alleys where everyday life unfolds, Gyeongsang-do brings together different eras and landscapes to tell a rich variety of stories. Beyond its famous attractions, following the unique cultures and natural scenery of each area reveals a side of Gyeongsang-do that feels both familiar and new.

In Gyeongsangbuk-do, discover Andong Hahoe Folk Village and Buseoksa Temple in Yeongju, where the lives and wisdom of past generations are preserved, as well as the historical treasures of Goryeong that bear traces of ancient Daegaya. The old Mungyeong Saejae pass, the dramatic peaks of Juwangsan in Cheongsong, and the lush forests of Bonghwa offer peaceful escapes into nature. From the blue East Sea coastline of Yeongdeok and Uljin to mysterious Ulleungdo Island and the star-filled night skies of Yeongyang, new scenery awaits at every turn.

In Gyeongsangnam-do, take an unhurried trip against the beautiful seascapes of Tongyeong and Namhae. At the foot of Jirisan Mountain, Sancheong, Hamyang, and Hadong offer tranquil scenery where nature and tradition meet. Upo Wetland in Changnyeong and the natural attractions of Geochang reveal an ecosystem that changes with the seasons. Long-standing cultural heritage sites, including Yeongnamnu Pavilion in Miryang, the ancient tombs of Haman, and Haeinsa Temple in Hapcheon, add depth to the journey.

Gyeongsang-do's appeal goes beyond scenery and heritage. Vibrant traditional markets, alleys filled with local stories, and distinctive cultural experiences and foods found only here all reveal a region's personality. These unique details woven into everyday life make a trip even more memorable.

Follow stories from the past, pause for a rest in nature, and experience local life. Discover your own special journey in the many corners of Gyeongsang-do.
