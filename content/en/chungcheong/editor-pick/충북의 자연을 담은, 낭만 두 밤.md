Experience a special 3-day, 2-night getaway in Yeongdong and Okcheon, Chungcheongbuk-do! 

Savor authentic local dishes,

### 01. Inteo Restaurant | Local Taste of Yeongdong, Olgengi-guk

Your first hearty meal upon arriving in Yeongdong!  
Why not kick off your trip with a bowl of authentic local comfort food?  
Have you ever heard of Yeongdong’s traditional specialty, **Olbaengi-guk**?

![Inteo Restaurant Photo 1](https://pds.joongang.co.kr//news/component/htmlphoto_mmdata/201710/05/6d277350-3881-48a2-a958-366b408ef44f.jpg)  


**Olbaengi** is the local Chungcheong-do dialect for *olgaengi*, or freshwater snails.

As a region enriched by clear waters, including the Geumgang River,  
Yeongdong has a rich culinary tradition of utilizing freshwater snails.

Slightly bitter yet deeply savory, olgaengi can be enjoyed in various dishes ranging from soup and seasoned salads to savory pancakes.  


![Inteo Restaurant Photo 2](https://d12zq4w4guyljn.cloudfront.net/750_750_20211122105404022_photo_15aff1843b38.webp)

![Inteo Restaurant Photo 3](https://ilovekfood.com/userfiles/board_file/816825_detail.jpg)

Above all, a warm, refreshing bowl of olgaengi haejangguk (soup packed with snails)  
is both nourishing and remarkably delicious!  

There are several specialized freshwater snail restaurants near Woryubong Peak.  
Among them, **Inteo Restaurant** is a renowned local favorite famed for its snail dishes!  

Having been featured on television multiple times, it's bound to spark your curiosity even more.  


**📍 Inteo Restaurant | Visitor Info**

- **Address:** 4, Sinchon 2-gil, Hwanggan-myeon, Yeongdong-gun, Chungcheongbuk-do
- **Inquiries:** 043-742-4525
- **Hours:** 08:00–20:00
- **Closed:** Every Tuesday
- **Parking:** Available


Take your first flavorful step into Yeongdong with a bowl of **Olbaengi-guk**, nurtured by pure nature!

### 02. Woryubong Trail | A Scenic Landscape Where the Moon Lingers


After lunch, let’s head out to explore Woryubong, one of Yeongdong's most picturesque natural spots!

![Woryubong Trail Photo 1](https://cdn.ardentnews.co.kr/news/photo/202509/7939_37729_5038.jpg)

Woryubong (月留峰) carries the romantic meaning, “where the moon pauses and lingers on the peak.”  
It is a famous landmark in Yeongdong boasting breathtaking scenery worthy of its name!  

The Woryubong area is home to 'Hancheon Palgyeong,' eight scenic views renowned for their timeless beauty since ancient times.  
Among them, Woryubong stands proud as the 1st scenic view, showcasing truly stunning landscapes.  

The best way to experience the charm of Woryubong up close is to take a slow, peaceful walk along the Woryubong Trail!  


**🌿 Guide to the 3 Sections of Woryubong Trail**

- **Section 1. Yeoulsori-gil** (Approx. 2.6–2.7 km)  
  A path to closely enjoy the sound of the Chogangcheon stream and rocky cliffs.
- **Section 2. Sansaesori-gil** (Approx. 3.2 km)  
  A leisurely stroll along a gentle, rustic countryside path.
- **Section 3. Punggyeongsori-gil** (Approx. 2.5 km)  
  A serene path through a pine tree forest near a tranquil temple.  


Another charm of the Woryubong Trail is how its scenery transforms with the seasons!  
From the fresh greenery of spring and summer, to autumn foliage, and pristine winter snowscapes.

Step away from the hustle and bustle, take a slow walk,  
and tune in to the sounds of the wind, the flowing water, and singing birds.  
Before you know it, your heavy mind will feel light and at ease.  

**📍 Woryubong Trail | Visitor Info**

- **Address:** Wonchon-ri, Hwanggan-myeon, Yeongdong-gun, Chungcheongbuk-do
- **Admission:** Free
- **Hours:** Open 24/7
- **Closed:** Open year-round
- **Parking:** Available


Now that you’ve spent a relaxing time surrounded by pristine nature,  
how about heading over to discover another facet of Yeongdong—its wine?

### 01. Cheonghakdong | Fragrant Mushroom Cuisine, Yeongdong's Specialty

Let’s kick off the second day at a leisurely pace!  

Yeongdong is well-known not only for grapes but also for top-quality shiitake mushrooms.  

With large temperature variations between day and night paired with clean natural surroundings,  
the mushrooms grown here feature thick textures and exceptional fragrance.

![Cheonghakdong 1](https://st.kakaocdn.net/shophow/p/E5271026738.jpg?ut=20260630172301&type=thumb&opt=R500x500.q90)

![Cheonghakdong 2](https://www.menutong.com/data/editor/2112/62d77da768bd44c188919bf878f686ed_1640589979_7387.jpg)

### 03. Busodamak | Breathtaking Scenery Spread Over Water

Okcheon is situated right next to Yeongdong.  
Located just about 30 minutes away by car from Yeongdong Station, it's wonderfully convenient to explore both regions together!  

![Busodamak 1](https://img.seoul.co.kr/img/upload/2011/11/23/SSI_20111123185938.jpg)

When you arrive in Okcheon, there is a landmark you simply cannot miss.  
It is **Busodamak**, a magnificent landscape stretching like a folding screen over Daecheong Lake!  

With a rocky cliff rising over 700 meters above the water,  
it inspired scholar Uam Song-siyeol of the Joseon Dynasty to praise it as "Little Geumgangsan (Small Diamond Mountain)" for its mysterious and majestic beauty.  


As the crowning jewel of the Chuso Palgyeong (Eight Scenic Views of Chuso), it naturally sparks your curiosity even further!  


If you want to fully appreciate the beauty of Busodamak,  
we highly recommend walking up to Chusojeong Pavilion!  

As you stroll along the walking path admiring the harmony between the lake and surrounding mountains,  
you will find yourself arriving at Chusojeong before you know it.  


![Busodamak 2](https://www.oc.go.kr/site/tour/images/contents/oc_cts4289_img03.jpg)  


Take in the mesmerizing harmony of rock ridges stretching across the water and the surrounding blue lake,  
and enjoy a leisurely time in Okcheon taking in this mystical, peerless scenery crafted by nature over long ages!  


**📍 Busodamak | Visitor Info**

### 01. Jangnyeongsan Natural Recreation Forest | Embracing Nature in the Forest and Cave!

Start your final day breathing in Okcheon's crisp, clean morning air!  

![Jangnyeongsan 1](https://cdn.ardentnews.co.kr/news/photo/202510/8117_38462_941.jpg)  

Your first destination is **Jangnyeongsan Natural Recreation Forest**, spreading across lush forests and valleys.  

Jangnyeongsan Natural Recreation Forest is a natural resting space where you can fully experience dense woods and fresh air,  
allowing you to relax your body and mind through forest bathing and peaceful walks.  


The recreation forest features diverse forest healing facilities equipped with heating systems,  
making it accessible even during mid-winter!  

With various hiking trails and walking paths established,  
it caters to everyone—from those seeking serious mountain climbing  
to those wanting a light stroll through woodland paths.  

![Jangnyeongsan 2](https://cdn.ardentnews.co.kr/news/photo/202610/21064_88500_4757.jpg)  

Did you know there is a special attraction in Jangnyeongsan besides its forest trails?  

It is none of other than the **Forest Cave** located right inside the recreation forest!  


This themed space was created by utilizing a former mine shaft,  
featuring colorful lighting and themed facilities spanning from Section 1 to Section 8 inside the cave.  


![Jangnyeongsan 3](https://www.telltrip.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/jangnyeongsan-forest-geumcheon-valley-camping3.webp)  


The cave interior maintains a cool, refreshing temperature of around 12–14℃ year-round.  
Visiting during the scorching summer months offers a uniquely refreshing escape!  


![Jangnyeongsan 4](https://tong.visitkorea.or.kr/cms/resource/56/4041056_image2_1.jpg)  


Exploring the diverse themed zones and experience facilities lets you discover local history alongside pure nature.  

Stroll along the lush forest paths,

### 02. Jinhansikdang | Okcheon's Local Flavors: Eotang-guksu & Doribangbang

Okcheon's regional delicacies—Eotang-guksu and Doribangbang—are absolute must-tries on this trip! 

Okcheon is famous for exceptional dishes utilizing freshwater fish!  
Among them, the stars you simply must taste are **Eotang-guksu** and **Doribangbang**!  


![Jinhansikdang 1](https://cdn.kndaily.co.kr/news/photo/201612/41852_33847_417.jpg)  


**Eotang-guksu** is a hearty noodle dish cooked in a deeply simmered broth made from ground freshwater fish.  
The deep, rich broth combined with chewy noodles provides a deeply satisfying and nourishing meal.  

![Jinhansikdang 2](https://i.namu.wiki/i/_jyca4tSYtCjg79etXknnvNliknIVyekhQNeHYjRkwW8nz_UmPpD2A2DRqJBIVP0IQFe3FS2FHW06UW8CZvHOA.webp)  

**Doribangbang** features small freshwater fish arranged in a neat circle on a frying pan,  
pan-fried until crispy, and then glazed with a sweet and spicy gochujang-based sauce.  

Because the fish are arranged in a round circle, it earned the cute name "Bangbang"!  

The crispy texture paired with the sweet and spicy sauce makes it an irresistible delicacy,  
and pairing it with Eotang-guksu creates the ultimate culinary combination!  


![Jinhansikdang 3](https://찐한식당.kr/data/file/gallery_menu/aa6a9c04cafb5be71d05af46115d824d_OX5QqnzI_6c3ba1f5c394a83f172bc3a8186a1f5cb7e02e3b.jpg)  


Experience the authentic local flavors of Okcheon at renowned spots like **Jinhansikdang**!
