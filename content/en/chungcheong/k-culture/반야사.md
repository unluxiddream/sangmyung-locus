Banyasa Temple in Nonsan is nestled below the impressive cliffs of Daedunsan Mountain. A cave formerly used as a limestone mine during the Japanese colonial period has been transformed into a Buddhist sanctuary, creating a mysterious yet peaceful atmosphere. Colorful media art inside the cave and unusual rock formations have provided a memorable setting for Korean dramas and films, including The Tale of Lady Ok. The combination of natural cliffs and contemporary installations offers unique sights and photo opportunities, while the quiet temple grounds invite reflection.
