Sunshine Land in Nonsan is a major filming location for Mr. Sunshine, the Korean drama that captivated audiences worldwide. This cultural tourism complex recreates the streets of early-20th-century Hanseong with remarkable attention to detail. At Sunshine Studio, visitors can walk past the Glory Hotel, tram tracks, and streets lined with traditional Korean houses and Western-style buildings, recalling iconic scenes from the series. A military experience center and 1950 Studio also bring different periods of Korean history to life, from the opening of Korea to the Korean War.
