Ocheon Port in Boryeong is a quiet harbor at the entrance to Cheonsu Bay on Korea's west coast, where calm waters and fishing boats create a peaceful maritime scene. On a hill above the port stands Chungcheong Naval Command Fortress, once a key center of Joseon-era coastal defense. From the Yeongbojeong Pavilion inside the walls, visitors can enjoy sweeping views of the harbor and sea. The port gained popularity as a filming location for the drama When the Camellia Blooms, and the historic surroundings evoke scenes from the show. Sample dishes made with fresh pen-shell clams, a local specialty, while enjoying one of the west coast's most inviting harbor destinations.
