Bueongsan Tunnel in Geumsan stands along a walking path beside the Geum River, opening onto magnificent natural views. Dramatic rocky cliffs and a nostalgic landscape made the area a filming location for series including All of Us Are Dead and The Uncanny Counter 2. The trail continues through the forest toward Bueongsan Mountain and crosses the Wolyeongsan Suspension Bridge over the Geum River, connecting to Wolyeongsan Mountain. The route offers expansive views and is one of Chungcheongnam-do's memorable nature attractions.
