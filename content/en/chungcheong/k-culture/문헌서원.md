Munheon Confucian Academy in Seocheon is a historic seowon dedicated to two great late-Goryeo scholars, Mogeun Yi Saek and Gajeong Yi Gok, whose portraits and spirit tablets are enshrined here. Its tranquil hanok buildings and crape myrtle blossoms have made it a filming location for period dramas such as Love in the Moonlight and The King's Affection. The grounds also offer hanok experiences, a traditional teahouse, and the Munheon Traditional Hotel. Surrounded by beautiful architecture and nature, it is a wonderful place to reflect on Korea's scholarly traditions and enjoy peaceful time away.
