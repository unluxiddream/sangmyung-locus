Beopjusa Templestay offers experiential and relaxation-focused stays at Beopjusa Temple in Boeun, a UNESCO World Heritage mountain temple. Activities include seated meditation, tea conversations with Buddhist monks, and walks along Sejo Trail. Surrounded by the impressive Palsangjeon Pagoda and the gilded Maitreya Buddha statue, visitors can escape everyday noise, breathe clear mountain air, and listen to birdsong. The program introduces the temple's long history and Buddhist culture while providing space for self-reflection amid the natural beauty of Chungcheongbuk-do.
