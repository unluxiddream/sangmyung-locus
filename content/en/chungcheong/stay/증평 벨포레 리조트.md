Belle Foret Resort in Jeungpyeong is a leisure complex surrounded by forests and a lake. Visitors can meet animals at the sheep farm, try mountain carts and activities at Moto Arena, or explore mini golf and a media-art center. A marina and lakeside paths offer beautiful natural scenery and opportunities to relax. With activities, entertainment, recreation, and food all in one place, the resort is an excellent destination for a full day of fun and rest.
