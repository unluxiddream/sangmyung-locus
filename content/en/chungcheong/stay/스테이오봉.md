Stay Obong is located on a quiet stretch of coast where guests can swim and explore tidal flats along a soft sandy beach. It is also a wonderful place to admire the West Sea's sunrise and sunset. Clean, carefully maintained interiors, an outdoor terrace for relaxing to the sound of waves, hotel-quality bedding, and attentive housekeeping make the stay comfortable. Enjoy a special break close to the sea.
