Island Resom in Taean is a premium seaside wellness resort facing Kkotji Beach on Anmyeondo Island, one of Korea's most celebrated sunset destinations. At its beachfront Oasis Spa, visitors can take in the stunning colors of the West Sea at dusk. Ocean-view rooms and a range of amenities make for a comfortable and restful stay. Natural coastal scenery and thoughtful relaxation come together for a special seaside experience in Taean.
