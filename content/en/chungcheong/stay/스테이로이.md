Stay Roy is a private hanok accommodation in Geumsan, Chungcheongnam-do, available to only one group per day. The host couple spent about a year personally restoring a house built in 1955, preserving its traditional charm while adding modern comforts. Guests have exclusive use of the main house, annex, and spacious lawn. A four-season indoor jacuzzi in the main house and an enclosed, climate-controlled barbecue area in the annex offer comfort whatever the weather. Walk along the nearby stream and enjoy the quiet village scenery of Geumsan—a perfect place to slow down and relax.
