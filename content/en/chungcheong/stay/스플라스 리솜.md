Splas Resom is a hot-spring water park resort in the Deoksan Hot Springs area, a destination with some 600 years of history. A year-round water park using natural Deoksan thermal water and a range of themed spas offer relaxation and entertainment. Thermal water also flows into every guest room, allowing guests to enjoy hot spring bathing in private. Comfortable accommodations, diverse dining options, and wellness programs add to the experience. Combining hot springs, leisure, and food in a natural setting, the resort offers a refreshing escape throughout the year.
