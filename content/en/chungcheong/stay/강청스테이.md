Gangcheong Stay is a charming red-roofed accommodation nestled between a clear stream and quiet fields in Iwon-myeon, Okcheon. From its garden, guests can take in the changing rural landscape through all four seasons and enjoy the calm, friendly atmosphere of a small village. The entire property is a private guesthouse with a main house and separate annex, a spacious yard, and a traditional wooden veranda. A jacuzzi, private barbecue facilities, and a fire pit for peaceful evenings add to the experience.
Spend quality time with family or a partner surrounded by beautiful countryside and the charm of traditional Korean architecture. At Gangcheong Stay, enjoy Okcheon's warm sunshine and a day away from the ordinary.
