My Summer Hanok in Gongju is a private traditional Korean house tucked into a friendly alley near Gongsanseong Fortress, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Classic hanok architecture blends with warm, modern interiors for a cozy, secluded retreat. Sanseong Market and Jemicheon Stream are nearby for leisurely walks, while an indoor jacuzzi adds to the relaxation. Sip tea in the garden courtyard or on the wooden veranda and experience the quiet charm of Gongju, once the ancient Baekje capital of Ungjin.
