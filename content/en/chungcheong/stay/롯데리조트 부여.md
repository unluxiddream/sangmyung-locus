Lotte Resort Buyeo is a large leisure resort that reinterprets Baekje royal-palace decorative painting and traditional architectural forms through contemporary design. Directly opposite Baekje Cultural Land, it offers easy access to the year-round Aqua Garden water park, a golf course, and Lotte Outlet for shopping. Comfortable guest rooms and convenient amenities make it a relaxing base for families and couples. Nearby historic attractions, including Baekje Cultural Land, Gungnamji Pond, and Busosanseong Fortress, let guests combine Baekje heritage with modern resort comfort.
