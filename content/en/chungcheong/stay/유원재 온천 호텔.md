Youonejae in Suanbo is a luxurious hot-spring hotel where guests can enjoy the natural thermal water of the historic 'royal springs' once favored by Joseon royalty, both in their rooms and in private open-air baths. Refined Japanese- and Korean-inspired gardens complement private bathing, beauty and wellness therapies, and carefully prepared dining. Secluded spaces, thoughtful rooms, and attentive service create a restful experience. Discover the heritage of Suanbo's hot-spring culture alongside modern luxury and enjoy a complete escape from everyday life.
