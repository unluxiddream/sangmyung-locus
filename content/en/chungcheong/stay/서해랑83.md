Seohaerang 83 is a private pool villa directly facing the coast in Dangjin, with open sea views and beautiful sunsets. A heated pool available year-round and barbecue facilities make it ideal for a relaxed stay with family or a partner. Swim while gazing across the sea, then watch the romantic colors of sunset spread over the West Sea. Modern architecture harmonizes with the coastal scenery to create a comfortable yet distinctive setting. It is a wonderful place to enjoy the beauty of Korea's western coastline.
