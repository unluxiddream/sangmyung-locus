Located within Yeongdong Rainbow Healing Tourist Complex, the Rainbow Healing Center is a leading wellness attraction in Chungcheongbuk-do. Inspired by the region's natural surroundings, its spaces explore themes of light, wind, water, and stone, including a light garden and meditation pond. Visitors can relax with a foot bath at the Healing Foot Spa and participate in a variety of restorative programs. A healing museum for children makes it suitable for families too. Leave everyday stress behind and enjoy a special blend of nature, art, and relaxation.
