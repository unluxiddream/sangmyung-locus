Jincheon Bell Museum is Korea's only specialist museum of Buddhist temple bells, showcasing their magnificent sounds and the history of traditional metal casting. Exhibitions feature reproductions of notable bells from different periods and explain the techniques used to make them. Visitors can even strike real bells in a hands-on area and hear their deep, resonant tones. The museum is a wonderful place to explore the cultural significance of Korean bells and the precision of traditional craftsmanship.
