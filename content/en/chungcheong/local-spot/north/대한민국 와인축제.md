Held annually in Yeongdong, a major Korean grape-growing area and center of domestic wine culture, the Korea Wine Festival is a celebration of local food and drink. Visitors can sample and purchase a wide variety of wines made by local wineries. Wine awards, wine-glass painting, visits to the Yeongdong Wine Tunnel, and colorful performances offer plenty of entertainment. Discover Yeongdong's distinctive wines and regional charm through an unforgettable culinary experience.
