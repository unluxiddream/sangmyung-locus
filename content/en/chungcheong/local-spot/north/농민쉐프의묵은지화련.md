Farmer Chef's Aged Kimchi Hwaryeon is a popular restaurant in Jincheon serving wholesome traditional Korean cuisine prepared with homegrown ingredients and carefully matured kimchi. Using patented aged-kimchi production methods, it creates distinctive flavors in dishes such as aged-kimchi rib hot pot and grilled pork with aged kimchi. Enjoy a generous meal that brings together fresh local produce and the rich flavors of traditional fermentation.
