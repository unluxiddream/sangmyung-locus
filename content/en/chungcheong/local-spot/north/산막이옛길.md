Sanmagi Old Road is one of Goesan's best-loved walking routes, created by restoring an old path to Sanmagi Village with environmentally friendly wooden boardwalks. Walk through pine forests beside the blue waters of Goesan Lake and admire nearby mountain scenery, including a viewpoint overlooking Korea's peninsula-shaped terrain and Deungjanbong Peak. The pristine forests and tranquil lake offer a welcome escape from everyday distractions and a chance to savor nature at an unhurried pace.
