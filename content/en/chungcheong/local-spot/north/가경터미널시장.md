Gagyeong Terminal Market is a traditional market near Cheongju's bus terminal. Stalls selling fresh fruit, vegetables, daily essentials, and local snacks make it easy to shop and sample regional flavors. Conveniently located close to the terminal, the market offers a lively glimpse into Cheongju's everyday life and friendly neighborhood culture. Browse the shops and discover simple pleasures among its many foods and sights.
