Sainam Rock is the fifth of Danyang's Eight Scenic Wonders, celebrated for its dramatic rock cliff rising some 70 meters. Its name is said to come from the scholar U Tak, who loved the scenery while serving as a government official (sain) during the Goryeo period. Red pines cling to the sheer rock face while a clear stream runs below, creating a landscape like an ink-wash painting. Enjoy a leisurely walk amid the impressive cliffs and peaceful beauty of the valley.
