U-dang Historic House in Boeun, formerly the Seon Byeong-guk House, is a traditional Korean residence built by the wealthy father-and-son pair Seon Yeong-hong and Seon Jeong-hun. Designated a National Folklore Cultural Heritage, the expansive 99-room complex includes a main residence, men's quarters, and an ancestral shrine. The house also preserves the family's philanthropic spirit and the history of Gwanseonjeong, a place established for educating younger generations. Today, it is used for hanok stays and wedding photography, offering visitors a fresh way to appreciate traditional architecture and its peaceful ambiance.
