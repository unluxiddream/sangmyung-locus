Darae Farm in Cheongju is an interactive dairy farm where visitors can make and taste fresh yogurt, cheese, and ice cream. Green pastures and charming buildings create a relaxing setting and lovely photo opportunities. Activities range from feeding hay to animals and milk to calves to learning how to make cheese. With experiences and treats for both adults and children, the farm is a wonderful place to make special memories with family or friends surrounded by nature.
