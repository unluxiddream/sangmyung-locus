Jwagusan Observatory in Jeungpyeong stands near the summit of Jwagusan Recreational Forest, offering clear views of stars and planets in the night sky. It is equipped with Korea's largest refracting telescope, measuring 356 mm in aperture, allowing visitors to observe nebulae, star clusters, galaxies, and other celestial objects in remarkable detail. A planetarium and exhibition facilities help explain astronomy and constellations. Discover the mysteries of the universe and make unforgettable memories under the stars.
