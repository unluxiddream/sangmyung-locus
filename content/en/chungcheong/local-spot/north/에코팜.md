Eco Farm in Jincheon is an educational farm where visitors can discover sustainable agriculture and experience a variety of rural activities. Programs include harvesting crops, walking on the soil, and interacting with farm animals and plants. Far from the pace of city life, it offers fresh air and time to relax in nature. Children gain valuable hands-on learning experiences, while visitors of all ages can make happy memories of the countryside.
