Heavenly Garden Aquatic Plant Learning Center in Okcheon is an ecological garden overlooking expansive Daecheong Lake. Lakeside trails, ponds growing water lilies and lotuses, and old-fashioned buildings create a distinctive, almost otherworldly landscape. Beautiful lake views can be enjoyed throughout the grounds, while the plant displays change with the seasons. It is a lovely spot for peaceful walks and quiet reflection amid the still waters and unusual natural scenery.
