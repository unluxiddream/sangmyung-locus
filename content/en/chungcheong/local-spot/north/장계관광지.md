Janggye Tourist Area in Okcheon invites visitors to stroll alongside the winding, clear waters of Daecheong Lake. Attractions include the 'Hyangsu 30-ri' walking trail, displaying illustrated poetry by Jeong Ji-yong, a viewpoint at Janggye Bridge, lotus gardens, and cafés. Gentle lakeside breezes and picturesque scenery create a quiet and relaxing atmosphere. Enjoy literature along the walking paths and sweeping views of the lake. It is a lovely place for peaceful reflection at the meeting point of nature and poetry.
