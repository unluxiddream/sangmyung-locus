Cafe 9981 is a stylish dessert café near Simcheon Station, with large windows offering expansive views of the surrounding countryside. Alongside carefully baked pastries and flavorful coffee, it serves creative desserts made with local Yeongdong peaches and dried persimmons. It is a relaxing place to enjoy the sweetness of regional ingredients while taking a break from the bustle of everyday life.
