Midongsan Arboretum in Cheongju is a provincial botanical garden where visitors can explore the rich plant and animal life along forest paths near Songnisan Mountain. Attractions include a forest science museum, forest ecology center, botanical gardens, trails, and greenhouses. Flowers, lush trees, and brilliant autumn foliage change with the seasons, offering beautiful walks year-round. Breathe in the crisp forest air and rediscover the pleasure of taking life slowly.
