Saenggeo Jincheon Healing Forest is a forest recreation area designed to promote physical and emotional well-being in a pristine natural setting. Its facilities include healing spaces, a charcoal and enzyme center, a streamside forest trail, a meditation area, a lawn, and a range of nature programs. Visitors can walk among dense trees, breathe fresh air, and enjoy the calm of the woods. With changing forest scenery throughout the seasons, it is one of Jincheon's most peaceful places to unwind and reconnect with nature.
