Gamasot Mat Galbi is a rib-specialty restaurant in central Yeongdong-eup, serving a variety of meat dishes including local Korean beef, pork ribs, and beef short ribs. Carefully marinated ribs in the restaurant's special savory sauce, tender Korean beef, and well-prepared side dishes come together for a satisfying meal.
