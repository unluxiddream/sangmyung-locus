Baeron Holy Ground in Jecheon is an important historic site of Korean Catholicism, named for its valley shaped like the bottom of a boat. It preserves the cave where Hwang Sa-yeong wrote his famous letter, the grave of Father Choe Yang-eop, and a church. The pond reflects brilliant red foliage in autumn, while each season brings its own beauty. Visitors of any faith can enjoy quiet walks and peaceful moments in this historically and spiritually significant place.
