Bokangcheon Poplar Forest in Jeungpyeong is a scenic riverside park where tall poplar trees form a leafy corridor beside colorful themed flower beds. Seasonal flowers, gentle evening lighting, and a windmill along the walking paths create a peaceful atmosphere. It is a wonderful place to picnic with children or stroll by the quiet water while enjoying a refreshing touch of nature in the city.
