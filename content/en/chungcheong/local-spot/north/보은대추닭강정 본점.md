The main branch of Boeun Jujube Dakgangjeong specializes in sweet, crispy chicken flavored with Boeun's premium jujubes blended into its secret sauce. Tender boneless chicken is fried until crisp, then coated in a sauce with the gentle sweetness and rich umami of jujubes. Choose from regular, spicy, soy sauce, and plain fried options. Easy to take away, it is a delicious local snack to enjoy while exploring Boeun.
