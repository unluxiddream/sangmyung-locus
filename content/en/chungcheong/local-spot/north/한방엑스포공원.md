Korean Medicine Expo Park in Jecheon was established to celebrate the city's rich medicinal-herb heritage and traditional Korean medicine. Its attractions include an herbal botanical garden, a medicinal-herb discovery center, herbal footbaths, and workshops for making medicinal infused spirits. Spacious paths and nature-friendly landscaping also make it a pleasant place to stroll. With opportunities to learn about local herbs and traditional healing practices, the park combines wellness, relaxation, and an introduction to Jecheon's unique cultural identity.
