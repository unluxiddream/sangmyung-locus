Danyang Gugyeong Market is a popular traditional market celebrated for foods made with the area's signature loess-grown garlic. Try garlic sundae, garlic dakgangjeong (sweet crispy chicken), garlic tteokgalbi, and garlic dumplings—favorites with culinary travelers. Located in central Danyang near the Namhan River, it is an inviting place to explore regional specialties, enjoy market snacks, and experience the town's bustling atmosphere beside its waterfront performance area.
