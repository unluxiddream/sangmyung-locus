Haedeun Roastery Café in Boeun-eup offers freshly roasted, aromatic coffee, sweet homemade desserts, and jujube tea made with the area's famous dates. Its bright and welcoming atmosphere is ideal for slowing down over a thoughtfully brewed cup of hand-drip coffee.
