Suanbo Hot Springs in Chungju is Korea's oldest naturally flowing hot-spring area, where thermal water is said to have emerged for some 30,000 years. According to tradition, King Taejo Yi Seong-gye visited these 'royal springs' to treat a skin condition. Mineral-rich water rises from about 250 meters underground at approximately 53°C, making it ideal for a relaxing soak. A pleasant footbath path along Seokmuncheon Stream offers free access, and nearby accommodations make it easy to extend a restorative hot-spring stay.
