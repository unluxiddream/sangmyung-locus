Seongnae-dong Gwanagol Gift Shop Street in Chungju took shape as young artists and entrepreneurs opened stores in the historic alleys around the former Joseon-era government offices. Its retro streets are lined with independent shops selling handmade accessories, designer stationery, independent publications, and eco-friendly gifts. Stroll past old hanok houses and friendly lanes to discover imaginative local souvenirs and distinctive products made in Chungju. It is an appealing spot for relaxed window-shopping and unexpected finds.
