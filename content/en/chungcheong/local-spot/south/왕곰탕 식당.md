Wang Gomtang Restaurant in central Buyeo-eup has specialized in gomtang, a rich beef soup, for 40 years. Its deeply flavored broth follows recipes passed down over the decades. Carefully prepared beef, tripe, and knee cartilage provide tenderness and savory flavor, complemented by fresh, seasonal vegetable kimchi. Enjoy a hearty meal in the welcoming atmosphere of Buyeo's traditional market district.
