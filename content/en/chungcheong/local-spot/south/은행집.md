Eunhaengjip specializes in handmade tofu and cheonggukjang (fermented soybean stew). Its dishes include house-fermented cheonggukjang, savory tofu made using seawater rather than conventional coagulants, black soybean tofu, and colorful three-variety tofu. All kimchi is made in-house. A generous meal of cheonggukjang, bibimbap, and wild vegetable side dishes from Chilgapsan Mountain captures the comforting flavors of country cooking.
