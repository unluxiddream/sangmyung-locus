Sabihyang is a Korean restaurant specializing in lotus dishes and lotus-leaf rice, located opposite Gungnamji Pond, a major attraction in Buyeo. Its signature lotus-leaf rice set features glutinous rice and mixed grains carefully steamed in fragrant lotus leaves, accompanied by dishes such as grilled beef patties and assorted seasoned vegetables. Enjoy a healthy Korean meal infused with the gentle scent of lotus and the traditions of Baekje.
