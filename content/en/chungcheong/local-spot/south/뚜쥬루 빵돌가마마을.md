Toujours Stone Oven Village is a celebrated local bakery in Cheonan, known for introducing one of Korea's first and largest bread-baking stone ovens. Its spacious grounds are dotted with charming, storybook-like buildings, and visitors can enjoy hands-on baking experiences involving muffins, doughnuts, and cakes. Guided by its 'slower is better' philosophy, the bakery minimizes chemical additives and carefully uses produce from the Cheonan area. Try signature treats such as stone-oven manju and turtle bread to discover the dedication and distinctive bread culture of Toujours.
