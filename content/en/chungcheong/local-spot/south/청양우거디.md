Cheongyang Ugeodi is a traditional Korean soup restaurant in central Cheongyang-eup, serving hearty haejangguk and gukbap made with fresh Korean-grown outer cabbage leaves and deeply flavored meat stock. Its signature dish is beef ugeojitang with locally grown goji berries, combining a rich, slightly spicy broth with tender greens. It is an inviting place for a wholesome and satisfying meal featuring Cheongyang's agricultural specialties.
