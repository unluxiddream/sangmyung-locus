The Yesan Samguk Festival brings together three of the town's favorite autumn attractions: chrysanthemums, noodles, and gukbap (rice soup). Visitors can admire colorful chrysanthemum displays and sample local dishes along Yesan's noodle and rice-soup streets. Food events organized by residents and merchants, performances, and hands-on activities add to the festive mood. Come savor the scent of autumn flowers and the comforting flavors of local cuisine.
