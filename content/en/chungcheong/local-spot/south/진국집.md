Jingukjip is a long-established Korean restaurant tucked away in a downtown Seosan alley, specializing in gegukji. This regional dish is traditionally made by fermenting cabbage kimchi in a clay jar with the marinade used for soy-marinated crab or brine from salted seafood, then simmering it in an earthenware pot. At Jingukjip, enjoy a spicy, deeply flavored gegukji meal made with aged kimchi and seafood, accompanied by rustic stews and carefully prepared side dishes.
