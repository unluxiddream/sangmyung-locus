Farmhouse Bakery is a long-established local shop in central Cheongyang, run by a master baker. Alongside baked treats, popular specialties include goji berry bread and aronia bread made with locally grown ingredients. The bakery also offers unusual creations inspired by the area's famous Cheongyang chili peppers, such as chili-filled chewy bread and chili baguettes.
