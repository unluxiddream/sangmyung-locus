Located near Buyeo Intercity Bus Terminal, Buyeo Jungang Market is a beloved traditional market that has been part of local life for generations. Its stalls offer Buyeo specialties and homemade dishes, including popular sundae soup, fried chicken, and ingredients for lotus-leaf rice. Stroll through the friendly market, enjoy the generosity of local merchants, and shop for regional products. The wealth of food and sights makes this an excellent place to experience Buyeo's everyday culture.
