Baekje Cultural Land in Buyeo is a large historical reconstruction complex designed to bring the history and cultural achievements of the ancient Baekje kingdom to life. Meticulously recreated sites include Sabi Palace, the royal residence of the Sabi era; Neungsa Temple, built to honor King Seong; a village illustrating homes of different social classes; and Wiryeseong Fortress. Wander among buildings inspired by historical research and feel a connection to Baekje's past. Traditional cultural activities also offer a vivid glimpse of its magnificent heritage and everyday life.
