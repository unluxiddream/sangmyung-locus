Oeam Folk Village in Asan is a traditional settlement with around 500 years of history, designated National Folklore Cultural Heritage No. 236. Well-preserved houses of upper-class families and ordinary residents from the late Joseon period provide a living museum of the time's social customs. Stone-walled lanes, traditional homes, and beautiful natural surroundings create a peaceful setting. Each October, the Straw and Reed Culture Festival explores traditional life-cycle rites and farming customs, while winter-solstice events are held in November. Visitors can also try rice-cake pounding and straw crafts to experience Korea's traditional ways of life firsthand.
