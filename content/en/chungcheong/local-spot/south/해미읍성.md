Haemieupseong Fortress in Seosan is a representative flatland fortress from the Joseon period, counted among Korea's three major town fortresses alongside Gochang and Naganeupseong. Admiral Yi Sun-sin once served here as a military officer, and the fortress also witnessed the martyrdom of many Catholics during the late-Joseon persecutions. A papal visit brought worldwide attention to its significance as a Catholic pilgrimage site. Visitors can stroll beside the walls and across broad lawns, visit a traditional teahouse, and enjoy cultural activities while exploring the area's layered history.
