Subuk-ro 1945 is a garden café in Gyuram-myeon, Buyeo, created by renovating a former Japanese-owned house dating to 1945. The historic, hanok-inspired atmosphere and cozy garden invite visitors to relax with drinks and brunch served in picnic baskets. Its menu features homegrown produce and flower teas, along with lotus-leaf rice, Subuk salad, and desserts using Buyeo strawberries. Guests can also enjoy activities such as feeding rabbits hay, making fruit syrups, and planting flowers, creating a special connection with nature.
