Pinnacle Land in Asan is a nature retreat featuring themed gardens and walking trails across approximately 99,000 square meters. Hydrangeas, chrysanthemums, tulips, and other flowers bloom in vibrant colors throughout the seasons, while weekly fireworks during festival periods add another highlight. Families and couples can also enjoy feeding alpacas and other friendly animals, as well as exploring a sculpture park. Beyond its scenic gardens and activities, Pinnacle Land promotes appreciation for plants and landscape design.
