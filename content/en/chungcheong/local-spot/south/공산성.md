Gongsanseong Fortress in Gongju served as the royal fortress when Ungjin was the capital of Baekje (475–538). It is a highlight of the Baekje Historic Areas, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Its walls stretch along the slopes of Gongsan Mountain above the Geum River, offering magnificent fortress views and sweeping river scenery. Explore historic structures such as Geumseoru and Gongbukru gates; at dusk, the illuminated walls take on a completely different charm. With history, nature, and beautiful night views in one place, Gongsanseong offers a memorable introduction to Baekje and Gongju.
