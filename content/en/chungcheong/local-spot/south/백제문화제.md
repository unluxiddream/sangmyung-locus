The Baekje Cultural Festival in Gongju reimagines the magnificent history and cultural heritage of Baekje 1,500 years ago through a contemporary lens. Held around Geumgang Singwan Park and Gongsanseong Fortress, it features illuminated traditional sailboats on the Geum River, media projections on the fortress walls, parades, and other engaging spectacles. Bringing together Baekje history, artistic traditions, and local culture, the festival offers a memorable bridge between the kingdom's past and present.
