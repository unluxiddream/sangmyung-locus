Nonsan Hwaji Jungang Market, in the heart of downtown Nonsan, is one of the largest traditional markets in southern Chungcheongnam-do. Alongside fresh seafood, produce, and regional specialties, visitors can enjoy local favorites such as sundae-gukbap, fried snacks, and rice cakes. The bustling merchants and their generous hospitality bring together everyday local life and long-standing traditions. With comfortable shopping facilities that can be enjoyed in any weather, it is a beloved destination for residents and visitors alike.
