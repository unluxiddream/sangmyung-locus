The Hansan Mosi Cultural Festival in Seocheon celebrates and preserves the long history of Hansan ramie weaving, recognized by UNESCO as Intangible Cultural Heritage. Around the Hansan Mosi Museum, visitors can watch skilled artisans weaving ramie cloth and take part in experiences such as the Hansan Mosi School, ramie-themed ASMR yoga, and making ramie wind chimes. Discover the craftsmanship behind this traditional textile and appreciate the beauty and cultural significance of Hansan ramie through a variety of activities.
