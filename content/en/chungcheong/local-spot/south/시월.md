Café Siwol is a dessert café on the craft-village street in Gyuram-ri, Buyeo. Warm wooden interiors and stylish details create a welcoming setting for carefully made cakes using seasonal fruit, along with aromatic tea and coffee. It is an ideal stop to savor changing seasonal flavors and enjoy the relaxed atmosphere of Buyeo's charming alleyways.
