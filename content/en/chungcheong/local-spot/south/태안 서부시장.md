Taean Seobu Market is a traditional market in central Taean-eup where visitors can find seasonal fish, oysters, blue crabs, six-clove garlic, and other local specialties fresh from the West Sea. Seafood stalls sell sashimi, spicy fish stew ingredients, and dried seafood at reasonable prices. Browse seasonal seafood and regional produce while soaking up the lively, friendly atmosphere of a west-coast market. It is a great place to discover Taean's distinctive local flavors.
