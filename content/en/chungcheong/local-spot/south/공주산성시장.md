Located close to Gongsanseong Fortress, Gongju Sanseong Market is a historic traditional market with a lively cultural atmosphere. It is famous for chestnut desserts made with Gongju's signature chestnuts, including chestnut mochi and chestnut bread, as well as traditional street food. On five-day market days (the 1st and 6th of the month), the marketplace comes alive with the charm of a rural market. Friendly vendors and bustling stalls offer a glimpse of local life. Browse unique Gongju foods and regional products and enjoy a relaxed shopping experience.
