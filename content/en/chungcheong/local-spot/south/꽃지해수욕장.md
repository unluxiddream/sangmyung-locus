Kkotji Beach on Anmyeondo Island in Taean is a beautiful stretch of sand backed by dense pine forests. Just offshore, the towering Halmi and Harabi rocks create a picture-perfect scene against the red glow of sunset, making this one of South Korea's best-known sunset spots. At high tide, enjoy the tranquil, romantic sea; at low tide, explore the exposed tidal flats and coastal paths. Nearby ATV and go-kart activities offer exciting ways to feel the sea breeze. From leisurely seaside walks to thrilling outdoor activities, Kkotji Beach is one of Taean's favorite coastal retreats.
