The Japanese-style House in Sinheung-dong, Gunsan, is a two-story wooden house built during the Japanese colonial period by Hirotsu Keisaburo, a Japanese man who ran a fabric shop and farms, and it is also known as the Hirotsu House. As it preserves the characteristics of an upper-class Japanese residence well, it was designated National Registered Cultural Heritage No. 183 in 2005, and it is now managed by Gunsan-si and open to the public. Numerous Korean films have been shot here, including <The General's Son> and <Tazza: The High Rollers>, as well as <Fighter in the Wind>, <Nameless Gangster: Rules of the Time> and <Gabi>. The Japanese-style garden and stone lantern laid out between the two buildings catch the eye, and you can look back on the lifestyle of Japanese landlords along with the history of the exploitation of rural Korea. However, as the interior is not open in order to protect the cultural heritage, please focus your visit on the exterior and the garden.
