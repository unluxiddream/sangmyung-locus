The Damyang Metasequoia-lined Road was created in 1972 when Damyang-gun planted 1,300 five-year-old metasequoia trees along National Route 24, and over the years it has grown into a soaring green tunnel. Standing in the middle of the road, you can capture a perfectly symmetrical composition of tree trunks on both sides, making it a popular photo spot. Known as a filming location for the films <Wanee & Junah> and <May 18> and the variety show <2 Days & 1 Night>, it draws many visitors who walk it while recalling scenes from those works. Refreshing greenery in summer, red autumn foliage in fall, and snow-covered scenery in winter give it a different atmosphere every season. It is a paid walking trail, and as operating hours vary by season, we recommend checking before your visit.
