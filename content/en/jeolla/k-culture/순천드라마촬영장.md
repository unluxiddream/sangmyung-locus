Suncheon Drama Film Set is Korea's largest film and drama set, recreating the streets of downtown Suncheon in the 1960s, the hillside shantytown of Bongcheon-dong in Seoul in the 1970s, and the streets of Seoul's outskirts in the 1980s. Around 80 productions have been filmed here so far, including the dramas <Love and Ambition>, <Giant>, <East of Eden> and <King of Baking, Kim Takgu>, as well as <Tale of the Nine Tailed 1938> and <Pachinko>. Recent hits such as <When Life Gives You Tangerines>, <Jeongnyeon: The Star Is Born> and <Our Unwritten Seoul> have also been filmed here, establishing it as a major hub for period-drama production. Walking through alleys lined with worn signboards and low-roofed houses along the slopes will remind you of scenes from these works, and you can also rent an old-style school uniform and take photos. Please note that on days of actual filming, some routes may be restricted.
