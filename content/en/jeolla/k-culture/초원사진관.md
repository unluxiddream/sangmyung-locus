Chowon Photo Studio is the filming location of <Christmas in August>, released in 1998, and the main setting of the film about the unfulfilled love between Jung-won (Han Suk-kyu), a photographer with a terminal illness, and Da-rim (Shim Eun-ha), a parking enforcement officer. The production team decided to film at a real location instead of a set and searched photo studios all over the country, eventually converting a garage shaded by summer trees into a photo studio for the shoot. The name Chowon Photo Studio is said to have come from the name of a photo studio in the neighborhood where Han Suk-kyu grew up, and the building, demolished after filming, was restored by Gunsan-si and is now open to the public free of charge. Inside, the camera, electric fan, albums and other props that appeared in the film are on display just as they were. Every year in late August, an alley festival commemorating the film is held in the area, where you can share memories of the film.
