Iksan Prison Film Set is Korea's only prison set for filming, built on the site of the closed Namseong Branch of Seongdang Elementary School. Starting with the film <Holiday> in 2005, some 200 films and dramas have been shot here, including <Miracle in Cell No. 7>, <Inside Men>, and the dramas <IRIS> and <Prison Playbook>. Posters of the works filmed here are displayed at the entrance, and spaces such as a courtroom, solitary cells, an interrogation room and a visiting room are recreated just like a real prison, so you may feel a chill as you take photos. Renting a prisoner's or prison guard's uniform and reenacting a scene from a film or drama is also a popular experience. As the set does not open on filming days, we recommend checking whether it is open before your visit.
