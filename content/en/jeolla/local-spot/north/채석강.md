Buan's Chaeseokgang is an area of coastal cliffs and rock beds stretching about 1.5 km from the right side of Gyeokpo Port to Dagibong Peak. The horizontally layered sedimentary strata are the Gyeokpo-ri Formation, formed about 70 million years ago in the Cretaceous period of the Mesozoic Era, and they make a striking sight, looking like countless books stacked on top of one another. Its name is said to mean that the scenery is as outstanding as the Red Cliffs (Jeokbyeokgang) where the Chinese poet Su Dongpo is said to have enjoyed himself. The sea caves and rock beds below the cliffs can be seen up close at low tide, so it is best to check the tide times before your visit; tide tables are available at the Byeonsanbando National Park visitor center. The sunset over the West Sea is considered the finest of the Eight Scenic Views of Byeonsan, and many people come at dusk.
