Jeonju Nambu Market is a traditional market that grew out of the early Joseon-era 'Nammun-bak Market' (market outside the South Gate) and took the name 'Nambu Market' after opening as a regular public market in 1905. Around 800 stores deal in silks, furniture, dried seafood, vegetables, fruit, medicinal herbs and more, and the 2nd floor of Market Building 6 is home to the Youth Mall run by young merchants. At the night market held every Friday and Saturday, you can enjoy distinctive foods such as pisundae (blood sausage) set meals, nokdujeon (mung bean pancakes) and bibimbap rolls. Along the Jeonjucheon Stream in front of the market, the Dokkaebi (Goblin) Market opens at 4–5 AM and is said to vanish once the day breaks, letting you see the bustle of local vendors and customers. Browsing the dawn market and then having breakfast at a Nambu Market restaurant is a route locals love.
