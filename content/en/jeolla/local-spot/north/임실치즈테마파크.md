Imsil Cheese Theme Park is a hands-on attraction themed on Imsil cheese, which originated when Belgian-born Father Didier t'Serstevens (Ji Jeong-hwan) began raising goats and making cheese. It covers about 130,000 ㎡ of meadow—the size of 19 soccer fields—in Doin-ri, Seongsu-myeon, Imsil-gun, and its European-style scenery makes you feel as if you have arrived in a Swiss village. You can take part in hands-on programs such as making Imsil cheese and rice-crust pizza, and the restaurants serve dishes made with cheese produced on site. In autumn it is famous for the sight of ten million chrysanthemums in bloom, and the Imsil N Cheese Festival is held here every year. As hands-on programs run at set times, we recommend checking the program schedule on the website before your visit.
