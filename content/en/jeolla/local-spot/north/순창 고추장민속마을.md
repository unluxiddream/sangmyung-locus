Sunchang Traditional Gochujang Folk Village is a fermented-sauce village built with hanok-style structures in the Baeksan-ri area of Sunchang-eup, and since its creation in 1997 it has become a space representing Korea's jang (fermented sauce) culture. Inside the village, skilled artisans of traditional jang-making produce and sell traditional foods such as gochujang (red pepper paste), doenjang (soybean paste) and ganjang (soy sauce). At the Sunchang Jang Museum in front of the village, you can learn about the history and making of gochujang and doenjang, and at the Sunchang Jang Experience Center you can take part in programs such as making gochujang and cooking with fermented sauces. Restaurants in the village also serve Korean dishes made with Sunchang local food and seasoned with Sunchang gochujang, so you can see, make and taste Sunchang's famous sauces all in one place.
