Gochang Eupseong Fortress is a town fortress built in 1453, the first year of King Danjong's reign in the Joseon Dynasty, and together with Ipamsanseong Fortress in Jangseong it served as a frontline base defending the inland Honam region. It is said to have witnessed three battles—during the Jeongyu War, the Donghak Peasant Revolution and the Korean War—and partially restored government office buildings remain inside, giving a glimpse of what the fortress town looked like at the time. It is a beloved walking course where you can stroll along the walls and look down on downtown Gochang and the surrounding scenery. Open from 5 AM to 10 PM, it is also ideal for an early-morning or evening walk. The Pansori Museum and the Old House of Sin Jae-hyo are nearby, making it a great place to explore Gochang's music and history together.
