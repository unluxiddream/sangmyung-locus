Gunsan's Lee Sung Dang is a bakery that began when a Korean took over 'Izumoya,' a Japanese confectionery shop run by a Japanese owner during the colonial period, after liberation and renamed it with its current name in 1945. It is known as the oldest bakery still operating in Korea and has also been certified as a 'Hundred-Year Store' by the Ministry of SMEs and Startups. Its signature items are the danpatppang, a thin bun generously filled with sweet red bean paste, and the yachaeppang, baked with cabbage and other vegetables—so popular that there is a separate line just for these two breads. You can also enjoy freshly made sandwiches and milkshakes. As it tends to be crowded on both weekdays and weekends, we recommend allowing plenty of time for your visit.
