Located around Geomasan-ro in Samcheon-dong, Wansan-gu, Jeonju, Samcheon-dong Makgeolli Alley is Jeonju's signature drinking alley, where long-established makgeolli houses gather along a stretch of about 200 meters. Around the 1990s, as makgeolli gained attention as Korea's traditional national drink, makgeolli houses gradually gathered in the existing Samcheon-dong alley to form the street as it is today, and it has been designated a Jeonju Future Heritage. The alley's biggest feature is that ordering just one kettle of makgeolli fills the table with more than twenty side dishes, such as grilled fish, jeon (savory pancakes), stew and fried foods. Each establishment has a slightly different atmosphere and lineup of dishes, so it is fun to pick the one that suits your taste, and lines sometimes form in the evening. It is a local food destination where you can experience up close the warm hospitality of Jeonju locals and their drinking-table culture.
