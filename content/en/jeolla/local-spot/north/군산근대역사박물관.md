Gunsan Modern History Museum is a specialized museum themed on Gunsan's modern and maritime culture, showing the city as it once was: a trading port and a hub of maritime logistics. The 1st floor houses the Maritime Logistics History Hall, the Children's Experience Hall and the Donation Exhibition Room; the 2nd floor has the Modern Archives Room and the Citizens' Open Gallery; and the 3rd floor is home to the Modern Life Hall and the Special Exhibition Room. Floor by floor, you can follow the thousand-year history of logistics and distribution on the West Sea and the story of Gunsan's growth into an international trading port. With the integrated ticket, you can also visit nearby modern architecture and art exhibition spaces, making it an ideal starting point for exploring Gunsan's modern-era streets. The museum parking lot is free of charge.
