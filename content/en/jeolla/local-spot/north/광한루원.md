Namwon's Gwanghalluwon is the garden of Gwanghallu Pavilion, created in the early Joseon period, and is known as the place where Chunhyang and Lee Mong-ryong first met in the Tale of Chunhyang. Gwanghallu is counted among Korea's four great pavilions along with Gyeonghoeru, Chokseongnu and Bubyeongnu, and Gwanghallu Pavilion (Treasure No. 281) is preserved together with the surrounding Gwanghalluwon garden (Scenic Site No. 33). Over the pond symbolizing the Milky Way lies Ojakgyo Bridge, which unites the legendary lovers Gyeonu and Jiknyeo, and islands modeled after the three sacred mountains sit in the pond, creating a landscape that seems to bring the heavenly world down to earth. The garden also contains Chunhyang Shrine, Wanwoljeong Pavilion and Wolmae's House, making it pleasant for a leisurely stroll, and the Chunhyang Festival is held every year around Dano. After 6 PM, admission is free and lights are added, offering an atmosphere quite different from the daytime.
