Seohak-dong Art Village, reached by crossing a single bridge from Jeonju Hanok Village, is an artists' village home to some 30 workshops large and small. The neighborhood, once called 'Seonsaengchon' (Teachers' Village) because many teachers and students lived there, was in decline when an artist couple settled here in 2010; painters, embroiderers, photographers and other artists gradually followed, shaping the village as it is today. The workshops, which make use of existing buildings, serve as both studios and homes, so you can catch glimpses of the artists' daily lives up close throughout the alleys. It is crowded with tourists during the day, but from around sunset you can wander the alleys in a much calmer atmosphere. As residents actually live in the village, please keep noise to a minimum and look around quietly.
