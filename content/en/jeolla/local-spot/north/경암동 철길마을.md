Gunsan's Gyeongam-dong Railroad Village formed along a roughly 2.5 km railway laid between Gunsan Station and a factory during the Japanese colonial period to transport newsprint. Until 2008, trains ran through the village twice a day, and the unique scenery of houses standing right up against the tracks made it a well-known photo spot. Today, along both sides of the disused tracks, murals line the way alongside shops where you can try old-fashioned treats and games such as making dalgona and grilling jjondeugi. At the end of the tracks there is also a photo spot with a train station and a train car. Parking can be congested on weekends and holidays, so we recommend using a nearby parking lot.
