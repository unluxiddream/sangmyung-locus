Sinan's Purple Island refers to Banwoldo and Bakjido, islands attached to Anjwado, called 'Purple Island' because the village roofs, railings and even bridges are decorated in purple. The 'Purple Bridge,' an approximately 1.5 km purple wooden bridge linking Anjwado, Bakjido and Banwoldo, originated from the lifelong wish of a grandmother in Bakji Village to be able to walk to the mainland, and the wooden bridge was built in 2007. You can experience walking over the sea from island to island, and in spring lavender blooms on Bakjido, where a lavender festival is held around May. The village has been selected as one of the Best Tourism Villages by the UN World Tourism Organization. Admission is waived if you wear any one of purple clothing, shoes, a hat or an umbrella, so preparing something purple in advance is a fun way to visit.
