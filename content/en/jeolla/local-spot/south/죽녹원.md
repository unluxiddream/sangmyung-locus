Damyang's Juknokwon is a dense bamboo forest of about 160,000 ㎡ located near Damyangcheon Stream and Gwanbangjerim Forest, opened in May 2003. A total of 2.2 km of walking trails winds through the bamboo groves, divided into eight themed paths such as Unsudaetong-gil (Path of Great Fortune), Jukmagou-gil (Path of Childhood Friends) and the Philosopher's Path. From the observatory, you can take in Damyangcheon Stream, the Gwanbangjerim Forest with trees over 300 years old, and even the Metasequoia-lined Road in a single view. Jungnokcha (bamboo-dew tea), said to grow on dew dripping from bamboo leaves, grows wild here, and lighting installed in the bamboo grove makes the atmosphere around sunset lovely as well. It is the landmark that symbolizes Damyang, the home of bamboo, perfect for walking slowly while listening to the wind rustling through the bamboo.
