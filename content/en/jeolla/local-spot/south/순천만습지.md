Suncheonman Bay Wetland is a coastal wetland spread across Suncheonman Bay, surrounded by Suncheon-si and the Goheung and Yeosu Peninsulas; known as one of the world's five great coastal wetlands, it is Scenic Site No. 41. A vast reed colony of about 5.4 ㎢ stretches from where Suncheon's Dongcheon Stream and Isacheon Stream meet all the way to the tidal flats, and walking along the reed deck and Mujingyo Bridge lets you observe the mudflats and migratory birds up close. From Yongsan Observatory, you can take in the S-shaped curving waterway glowing golden at sunset in a single view, making it one of the most celebrated photo spots. Riding the eco-experience boat in the wetland lets you see migratory birds even more closely along the tidal channels, and Suncheonman Bay Astronomical Observatory runs night-sky observation programs. It is Suncheon's signature eco-tourism destination, where you can spend a day watching birds by day and stars by night.
