Cheongsando is an island about 19 km from Wando Port, so named because its sky, sea and mountains are all blue-green ('cheong'). It became part of Dadohaehaesang National Park in 1981 and in 2007 was designated Asia's first Slow City. The Slow Trail connecting every corner of the island consists of 11 courses and 17 paths totaling 42.195 km, letting you walk slowly past the blue sea, stone-wall lanes and gudeuljang-non (terraced rice paddies built on flat stones). It is also known as a filming location for the film <Seopyeonje> and the drama <Spring Waltz>, and there are other attractions such as Beombawi Rock and Dolmen Park. A Slow Walking Festival is held every spring, and we recommend checking ferry times from Wando Port before your visit.
