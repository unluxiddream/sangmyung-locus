Jindo's Unlimsanbang is the studio where Sochi Heo Ryeon, hailed as a master of Southern School literati painting in the late Joseon period, devoted himself to painting after leaving life in Seoul and returning to his hometown. Its name comes from the mist that rises morning and evening in the deep valley at the foot of Cheomchalsan Mountain, forming a 'forest of clouds.' The ㄷ-shaped tile-roofed studio, the thatched-roof living quarters behind it, and a pentagonal pond with an island holding a crape myrtle tree in its center create a charming, intimate landscape. The Sochi Memorial Hall displays works by three generations of the Heo family, and at the Namdo Traditional Art Museum near the ticket office you can view ink-wash landscape paintings free of charge. As the entrances to Ssanggyesa Temple and Unlimsanbang are close to each other and easy to confuse, please keep this in mind when visiting.
