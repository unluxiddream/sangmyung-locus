Suncheon's Naganeupseong is where the Joseon-era fortress walls, government offices, guesthouse and thatched-roof houses are preserved close to their original form, making it the first place in Korea where a fortress and its village together were designated a Historic Site; it is currently Historic Site No. 302. Fortress walls about 1,410 m in total length surround three villages—Dongnae, Namnae and Seonae—and around 100 households still actually live inside the walls today. Because it remains just as ordinary commoners lived, rather than as an aristocratic yangban village, you can encounter the simple residential scenery of Namdo, where thatched roofs, stone walls, wooden porches and jangdokdae (earthenware jar terraces) come together. Walking along the top of the walls, you can take in a panoramic view of the thatched-roof village, and it is also known as a filming location for many historical dramas and TV series. Various folk events are held throughout the seasons, so we recommend checking the schedule before your visit.
