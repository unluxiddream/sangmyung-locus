Naju Gomtang Hayanjip is a long-established Naju gomtang (beef soup) restaurant that started in 1910 as 'Ryumun Restaurant' at the market by the Naju Moksa Naea (magistrate's residence) and has continued for more than 110 years; it is now run by master Gil Hyeong-seon, the fourth generation to carry on the family business. The broth is made by simmering it for three hours starting at 2 AM and then skimming off fat and impurities for another three hours, using Korean hanwoo beef. Unlike the milky gomtang made by long-simmering beef bones, Naju gomtang is characterized by a clear, clean-tasting broth made mainly from meat cuts such as brisket and shank. The signature dishes are gomtang and suyuk gomtang, which comes with more generous portions of meat, and the tender suyuk (boiled beef slices) is also very popular. The open kitchen lets you watch the cooking process, so you can eat with confidence, and lines sometimes form at mealtimes.
