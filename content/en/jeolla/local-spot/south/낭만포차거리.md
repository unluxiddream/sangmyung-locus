Yeosu's Nangman Pocha Street is a street of pojangmacha (tented food stalls) set up in the plaza beneath Geobukseon Bridge, where you can enjoy food and drinks while taking in the night view of the Yeosu sea. Eighteen pocha stalls currently operate, offering a wide range of dishes from seasonal fare to unique seafood dishes found only in Yeosu. Busking and other performances are held regularly, adding even more to the atmosphere of the night sea. Business hours vary by season—from November to February it is open from 5 PM to midnight—so we recommend checking before your visit. Combined with nearby Yi Sun-sin Square and Jinnamgwan Hall, it makes an ideal night-walk course through Yeosu's old downtown.
