Suncheonman Sup is a detached-house hanok pension located about 1.4 km from Suncheonman Bay Wetland, where you can relax in the fresh air while walking the trails through the surrounding pine forest. All nine buildings are detached private units, guaranteeing privacy, and you can choose between an ondol (heated-floor) room and a bed room according to your preference. Each room has its own terrace and barbecue area, and in summer some rooms offer a children's pool. You can also enjoy traditional games such as yutnori and jegichagi, making it a Korea Quality certified accommodation well suited to families.
