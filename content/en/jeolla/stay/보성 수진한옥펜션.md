Sujin Hanok Pension is a traditional hanok stay in a hanok village near Daehan Dawon, the largest green tea plantation in Boseong, the home of green tea. With the tea fields, mountains and the sea at Yulpo Beach close by, it is a great base for enjoying Boseong's nature, and it is equipped with facilities such as a barbecue area, a jjimjilbang (Korean sauna) and a summer-only swimming pool. Some rooms include breakfast, and with advance reservation it also offers transport between the accommodation and the tea fields, as well as morning drop-offs to the station or bus terminal. It is a stay well suited to families, couples and groups alike.
