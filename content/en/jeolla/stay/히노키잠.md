Hinoki Jam is a guesthouse in the heart of Gunsan's Modern Culture and History Street, housed in a renovated two-story traditional Japanese-style wooden house. Inside the building you can feel firsthand the history of Gunsan, a port city during the Japanese colonial period, so it is popular with travelers exploring the Time Travel Village on foot, including the Japanese-style House in Sinheung-dong, Dongguksa Temple and Chowon Photo Studio. There are seven rooms ranging from doubles to rooms for up to eight people, all with en-suite bathrooms, and toast and drinks are served in the morning.
