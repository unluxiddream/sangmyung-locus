Seola Dawon is a tea plantation and hanok stay at the foot of Duryunsan Mountain in Haenam, cultivated by a couple who are gugak (traditional Korean music) performers. So that guests who come out of a love of tea can stay overnight, they built and run several houses—a stone house, an earthen house and a rammed-earth house—and wide green tea fields and walking paths spread around the accommodation, letting you rest amid the scent of tea. With advance reservation, you can enjoy a tea ceremony experience tasting organic tea along with a pansori performance by the owners, and programs such as gugak experiences and walks through the tea fields are also offered. It is an accommodation certified under the Korea Quality tourism certification.
