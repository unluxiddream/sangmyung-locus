Manginidang Historic House is the Kim Seon-gi House, designated as a Jeollanam-do Cultural Heritage Material, and is a historic-house stay in Damyang where you can stay in a traditional house built some 420 years ago. Because the original layout has been preserved, the bathroom and shower are outside the rooms, which may be a little inconvenient, but its charm lies in letting you experience the life of a Joseon-era yangban (aristocratic) household as it was. In the spacious garden you can barbecue over oak and bamboo charcoal or enjoy 'bulmeong' (gazing into a fire), and children can run around freely, making it especially good for families. Dogs are welcome, and Changpyeong Market and Samjinae Village in the Slow City of Changpyeong are a short drive away.
