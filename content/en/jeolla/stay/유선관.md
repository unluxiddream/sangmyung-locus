Yusungwan is a hanok inn located at the foot of Duryunsan Mountain in Haenam, on the forest path leading to Daeheungsa Temple, with a long history of serving as an inn since 1914. You can feel the atmosphere of the Daeheungsa area, intertwined with Master Choui's tea culture and the stories of Dasan Jeong Yak-yong and Chusa Kim Jeong-hui, and today it operates six hanok rooms that have been renovated in a modern style. Each room comes with a space for enjoying tea and a small courtyard, and the inn has a tea room where you can taste Haenam green tea, a private spa overlooking the forest, and a forest café that doubles as a breakfast space.
