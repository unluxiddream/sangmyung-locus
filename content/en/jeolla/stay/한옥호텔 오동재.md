Odongjae is a hanok hotel facing the Yeosu sea that opened in 2012; it became known for providing accommodation during the Yeosu World Expo and was selected as a 'Star of Korean Tourism' in 2014. Its signature feature is that the front of every room is made of glass, so you can enjoy the sunrise, Odongdo Island and the Yeosu night sea from inside your room. It has 40 rooms in both Korean (ondol) and Western styles, as well as an outdoor traditional wedding venue and a banquet hall, making it a great place to enjoy a seaside trip in the atmosphere of a traditional hanok. Pick-up is also available upon prior inquiry.
