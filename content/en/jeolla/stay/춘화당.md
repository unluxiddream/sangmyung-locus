Chunhwadang is a modern hanok guesthouse in the old downtown between Mokpo Station and Yudalsan Mountain, converted from a former herbal medicine shop built in 1929. The structure of the modern-era hanok, including its corridor-style toenmaru (wooden porch) and decorative glass windows under the eaves, is well preserved, and it has been designated a Mokpo-si cultural heritage and has also received the Korea Tourism Organization's Hanok Stay certification. It consists of the main house, an annex overlooking Yudalsan Mountain, and a small garden full of spring flowers, and you can enjoy breakfast and a welcome tea at the café at the entrance, which bears the 'Chunhwadang Herbal Medicine Shop' signboard. It is well located for exploring old downtown sights such as the Mokpo Modern History Museum, Nojeokbong Peak and Yudalsan Mountain on foot.
