Injae Gotaek Hakindang is the oldest historic-house cultural heritage in Jeonju Hanok Village, an accommodation where you can stay in a building constructed by artisans of the late Joseon (Korean Empire) era following royal palace architectural styles. In addition to the guest rooms of the Yeongbingwan, its signature space, there is a detached sarangchae with rooms on both sides of a central daecheong (wooden-floored hall), ideal for families, and in the byeoldangchae, divided into three spaces, you can spend the evening gazing at the traditional garden as it lights up at night. It is also equipped with a heritage lounge and a banquet hall, and it is well located for exploring the alleys of the Hanok Village, Gyeonggijeon Shrine and Jeonju Hyanggyo on foot.
