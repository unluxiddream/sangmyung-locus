Soyang Gotaek is an accommodation and multicultural space based on a 180-year-old traditional hanok in Oseong Hanok Village, Wanju. It preserves the structure and history of the old hanok while adding a modern sensibility, and alongside the accommodation it runs Dube Café, the independent bookstore Flicker and Dube Gallery, so you can enjoy exhibitions, book talks and local experience programs during your stay. The garden, which changes with each of the four seasons, harmonizes with the scenery at the foot of Jongnamsan Mountain, and it is also known as a drama filming location. It is an accommodation certified under the Korea Quality tourism certification.
