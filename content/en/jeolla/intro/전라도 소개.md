Located in the southwestern part of Korea, Jeolla-do is a land of abundance where vast plains, countless islands and beautiful nature come together with deep traditions of art and cuisine. Its name comes from the first syllables of Jeonju (全州) and Naju (羅州), two of the leading towns of the Joseon Dynasty, and it is also called the 'Honam (湖南) region,' meaning the land south of the Geumgang River. Blessed since ancient times with fertile granaries and clean seas, the region has always been rich in produce, which allowed a diverse food culture and the arts to flourish.

Jeollabuk-do stands out for its living traditions and magnificent mountains. At Jeonju Hanok Village you can experience the unique beauty of Korea, while the Mireuksa Temple Site in Iksan and the dolmen sites of Gochang preserve the traces of a long history. The majestic landscapes of Jirisan and Deogyusan Mountains offer ever-changing scenery through all four seasons, and the Saemangeum area shows the dynamism of Jeollabuk-do as it leaps toward a new future.

Jeollanam-do is a region where the spectacular scenery of the Dadohae archipelago, spread along a ria coastline, meets a treasure trove of ecosystems. Suncheonman Bay Wetland, with its world-class ecological value, the green tea fields of Boseong, and the romantic seascapes of Yeosu and Mokpo offer travelers deep relaxation. From the bamboo forests of Damyang to Ttangkkeut Village in Haenam, wherever you go you will find the generosity of nature and a quiet, unhurried calm.

Above all, what makes Jeolla-do special is its deep cultural soil, worthy of its nicknames 'Yehyang (藝鄕),' the home of the arts, and 'Mihyang (味鄕),' the home of flavor. The lineage of traditional arts such as pansori and Namdo folk songs lives on, and Jeonju bibimbap, tteokgalbi, and Namdo hanjeongsik—a table overflowing with seafood and jeotgal (salted fermented seafood)—show the region's generous hospitality and authentic taste. Fresh ingredients from fertile land and sea, combined with careful, heartfelt cooking, make Jeolla-do's charm shine even brighter.

From endless open fields to islands embroidering the sea and bountiful tables full of human warmth, Jeolla-do is a destination where rich flavor and beauty permeate every step. Explore every corner of Jeolla-do, where tradition, nature and art blend in harmony, and create a journey that is truly your own.
